Orbit Dash

Orbit Dash: One-Tap Arcade is a neon arcade game for iPhone. Tap to switch orbits, dodge walls, chain gems and earn 3 stars across 30 levels in 5 worlds.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Tap anywhere on the screen to jump between the inner and outer orbit. Avoid the glowing walls and collect gems. Collecting gems in a row builds a combo of up to 8x.
How do I get 3 stars?
Finish the level to earn 1 star. Collect at least half the gems for 2 stars, and nearly all of them for 3 stars.
How do I unlock the next world?
Finish every level in a world to open the next one.
What is the Daily Challenge?
Each day there is one new course, and it is the same for every player. Compare your score on the Game Center daily leaderboard.
Leaderboards aren't showing.
Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app (Settings → Game Center), then reopen Orbit Dash.
Can I turn off the sound?
Yes. Tap the speaker button in the top-right corner.
Will I lose my progress?
Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
