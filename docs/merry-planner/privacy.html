Merry Planner Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Merry Planner ("the app") is made by Justin Hemphill. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Information stored on your device

The names, gift lists, budgets, birthdays, notes, photos and other details you enter are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use Save backup, the backup goes wherever you choose to send it through the iPhone share sheet.

Photos and camera

If you add a photo to a gift, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library. The photo is resized and kept on your device only.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent to a server to deliver them.

Purchases

Merry Planner Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple only whether Premium has been purchased.

Links to stores

Gift ideas can open search pages on Amazon, Walmart and Target. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Some links may include an affiliate tag so that Merry Planner may earn a commission on qualifying purchases. As an Amazon Associate, Merry Planner earns from qualifying purchases. We do not receive information about who you are or what you buy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
