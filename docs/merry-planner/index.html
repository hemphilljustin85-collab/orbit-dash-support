🎄 Merry Planner

Merry Planner is a Christmas planner for iPhone: gift lists for everyone you shop for, gift ideas with store links, a gift budget, a Christmas savings fund, shopping and to-do lists, Secret Santa, birthdays and occasions, and printable gift tags.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes your data, so use Extras → Settings → Save backup to keep a copy.
How do I move my lists to a new phone?
On the old phone, go to Extras → Settings → Save backup and send it to yourself (Notes, Messages or email). On the new phone, go to Extras → Settings → Restore and paste it.
What does Premium include?
A one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited people (the free version holds 3), Secret Santa, birthdays and occasions with reminders, printable gift tags, and gift photos. It is not a subscription.
I bought Premium. How do I get it on another device or after reinstalling?
Open Extras → Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Do the store buttons cost me anything?
No. Gift ideas link to Amazon, Walmart and Target search pages. Merry Planner may earn a small commission if you buy something, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, Merry Planner earns from qualifying purchases.
Reminders aren't showing up.
Open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Merry Planner and turn on Allow Notifications. Then turn reminders on in Extras → Settings or Extras → Occasions.
How do I print gift tags?
Go to Extras → Gift tags, choose a style, tap Make tags, then Print or share. Choose Print from the share sheet. Cardstock works best.
