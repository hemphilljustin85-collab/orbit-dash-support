Privacy Policy

Orbit Dash: One-Tap Arcade · Effective date: November 16, 2026

Orbit Dash is an offline arcade game. We built it so that we do not collect your personal information.

Information we collect

None. Orbit Dash has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. Your level progress, stars, best scores and settings are stored only on your device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, Orbit Dash sends your scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles Game Center under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your Game Center data on any server.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, the iOS share sheet opens with a short text summary of your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Tracking, ads and sale of data

Orbit Dash does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not show third-party advertising, and does not sell personal information.

Children

Orbit Dash is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all locally saved progress. To remove Game Center scores, manage your Game Center account in the iPhone Settings app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
